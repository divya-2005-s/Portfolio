ClauseBerry

Terms of Service analysis and policy change monitoring platform

Tracks how apps' privacy policies and terms of service change, explains each meaningful change in plain English with a documented 1–5 risk score, and answers questions with citations to the exact policy text.

  • Built a change-detection pipeline over 2,264 archived policy versions from 26 services, separating 485 meaningful wording changes from 2,174 version pairs.
  • Created a word-level diff viewer that compares any two versions of a policy side by side, with risk reasoning and evidence quotes.
  • Implemented cited question answering with retrieval over ChromaDB and sentence-by-sentence tracking of AI-training clauses.
  • Covered the FastAPI backend with 63 pytest tests and the Next.js app with Playwright end-to-end tests, and automated a daily pipeline with GitHub Actions.

EV charging infrastructure and vehicle registration data analytics

An electric mobility analytics platform built on VAHAN vehicle registration data and Government of India charging-station data, showing EV adoption by state and where registrations outpace public charging.
